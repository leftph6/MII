## Evidence

BDLOB combines a limit-order-book deep model with Bayesian dropout to expose predictive uncertainty. The important transferable interface is calibrated uncertainty and abstention, not the original order-book tensor.

## Relevance

Provides a design precedent for posterior confidence, OOD checks and fail-closed output in the inference layer.

## Limitation

Uncertainty estimates require calibration and do not make an unobservable intent label factual.
