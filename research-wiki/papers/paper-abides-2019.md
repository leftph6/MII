## Evidence

ABIDES presents an agent-based, message-driven market simulator with configurable agents, exchange logic and latency. It is useful as an architectural reference for event-driven multi-agent experiments.

## Relevance

The BSC simulator should borrow event scheduling, agent interfaces and replay/calibration ideas while implementing CFMM reserve transitions and transaction ordering.

## Limitation

ABIDES targets CLOB-style markets and cannot be treated as a PancakeSwap simulator without a mechanism-specific adapter.
