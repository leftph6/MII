## Evidence

FinRL provides an open-source finance reinforcement-learning framework with environment, agent and training layers plus transaction-cost and risk-control examples.

## Relevance

It is a useful baseline organization and reporting reference for the project server, not a source of BSC data or an AMM execution model.

## Limitation

Stock/portfolio defaults, reward choices and market assumptions must be replaced for BSC meme-coin pools.
