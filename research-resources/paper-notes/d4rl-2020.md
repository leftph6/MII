## Evidence

D4RL frames offline reinforcement learning around static datasets, behavior-policy support and evaluation under distribution shift. Its central warning is that a policy can exploit unsupported actions in the dataset.

## Relevance

Supports using logged BSC trajectories, behavior-policy coverage, action-support diagnostics and conservative policy evaluation before any deployment claim.

## Limitation

D4RL benchmark environments are not financial markets and do not solve label quality, AMM execution or chain data completeness.
